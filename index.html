What We Do

Six Ways We Protect Your Business

No bloated packages. No jargon. Just the services that actually matter.

01

Website Security Audit

We scan your website for vulnerabilities and deliver a clear, plain-language report with exact steps to fix every issue found.

  • SQL injection & XSS testing
  • SSL / HTTPS verification
  • Outdated plugins & CMS checks
  • Security headers analysis
  • PDF report with fix steps
02

Password & Account Security

Most breaches start with a weak or reused password. We audit your access controls and harden them before attackers find the gap.

  • Password policy review & setup
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA)
  • Leaked credential checks
  • Password manager guidance
  • Shared account risk assessment
03

Email Security Setup

Stop attackers from impersonating your domain. We configure the technical records that make your email trustworthy and hard to spoof.

  • SPF, DKIM & DMARC setup
  • Domain spoofing check
  • Anti-spam filter configuration
  • Business email best practices
  • Phishing exposure assessment
04

Phishing Awareness Training

Your team is your biggest vulnerability. One well-timed click can bring down an entire operation. We make sure your people know exactly what to look for.

  • 60-min live training session
  • Real phishing examples & red flags
  • What to do if you click a link
  • Security do's & don'ts handout
  • Remote or in-person delivery
05

Data Backup Assessment

If ransomware hits tomorrow, can your business recover? We find out — and help you fix it before it ever becomes a question that matters.

  • Backup strategy review (3-2-1)
  • Recovery reliability testing
  • Cloud vs. local recommendation
  • Automated schedule setup guidance
  • Disaster recovery action plan
06

Monthly Security Check-in

Ongoing protection on a simple monthly retainer. We monitor, update, and report so security stays completely off your plate.

  • Software & plugin update checks
  • Login activity review
  • New vulnerability alerts
  • Monthly summary report
  • Direct support line
