Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with Aegis Security.

Is cybersecurity really necessary for small businesses?

+

Yes — and significantly so. 43% of cyberattacks specifically target small and mid-size businesses, largely because they are perceived as easier targets with fewer defences. The financial and reputational damage from a breach — including data loss, downtime, and client trust — can be severe. Most attacks succeed not because defences are impossible to build, but because none were in place. Aegis makes professional-grade protection accessible and affordable.

How much do your cybersecurity services cost?

+

Your first security assessment is completely free — no credit card, no catch. Our paid services are priced to be genuinely accessible for small and mid-size businesses, well below typical enterprise security rates. Contact us and we'll provide a clear quote based on your specific needs.

How quickly do I receive my audit report?

+

We deliver a complete, plain-language PDF report within 48 hours of beginning your security assessment. The report covers every vulnerability found and provides exact, prioritised steps to fix each issue — no IT background required to act on it.

What is DMARC and why does my business need it?

+

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting and Conformance) is an email authentication standard that prevents attackers from sending emails that appear to come from your domain. Without it, scammers can impersonate your business to target your clients or staff. Aegis configures SPF, DKIM and DMARC as part of the Email Security Setup service.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

+

No. Aegis has no lock-in contracts of any kind. Individual services are available as one-time engagements. If you want ongoing protection, our monthly check-in retainer runs month-to-month — you stay because the service is useful, not because you're contractually obligated.

Can phishing training be delivered remotely?

+

Yes. Our 60-minute phishing awareness training session is available both remotely (via video call) and in person. It covers real-world phishing examples, how to spot red flags, what to do if someone clicks a suspicious link, and practical security habits for your whole team.
